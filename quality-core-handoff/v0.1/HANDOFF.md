# Zlet Converter Quality Core Handoff v0.1

Handoff profile: `zlet-converter-quality-handoff/0.1`

This is the first bounded product handoff of `Zlet.Quality.Core`. It qualifies a subset of deterministic checks for direct in-process use by Zlet Converter. It does **not** claim that every metric exposed by the library is product-qualified.

## Frozen identities

- Quality Core: `0.1.0`
- embedding API: `zlet-quality-core-api/0.1`
- comparator: `zlet-exact-quality-comparator/0.4.0`
- metrics: `conversion-fidelity/0.4.0`
- CQS methodology: `zlet-cqs/0.3.0`
- product qualification profile: `zlet-converter-quality-handoff/0.1`

The exact exported source commit and per-file SHA-256 values are generated by `scripts/export_quality_core_handoff.py` after merge. Zlet Converter must retain that export manifest unchanged beside the vendored snapshot.

## Product boundary

The handoff contains only the reusable `.NET 8` Quality Core library, its normal NuGet library dependencies, the qualification profile and product-safe regression fixtures/contracts.

It does not transfer Python, Docker, benchmark orchestration, competitor runtimes, evidence-run storage, or external reviewer dependencies into Zlet Converter.

The intended direct entry point remains:

```csharp
await new QualityEvaluationService().EvaluateAsync(sourcePath, markdownPath, cancellationToken);
```

Zlet Converter owns product status mapping and presentation. SourceFacts/MarkdownFacts payload text must not be copied into user-facing product reports by default.

## Qualified product subset

The machine-readable authoritative matrix for this handoff is `handoff/zlet-converter-v0.1/qualification.json`.

### TXT

Bounded qualification covers exact content retention/noise, empty-output Integrity, exact coverage, and pairwise order when enough exact anchors exist.

### DOCX

Bounded qualification covers content recall, heading/list/table presence, heading/list hierarchy, table-cell position, order where exact anchors are available, empty-output Integrity and exact coverage.

Precision/noise interpretations that depend on table-header representation are deliberately excluded from product qualification.

### PPTX

Bounded qualification covers exact content and heading/list properties supported by current SourceFacts, empty-output Integrity and exact coverage. Reading-order claims remain subject to inspector certainty and may be `NOT_EVALUATED`.

### XLSX

Bounded qualification covers exact cell/content recall, cell positions, order where exact anchors exist, empty-output Integrity and coverage. Precision/noise claims are excluded because worksheet-name metadata and numeric display equivalence are not fully represented.

### PDF

Only coverage and explicit `NOT_EVALUATED` behavior are handed off. Current PDF SourceFacts are inferred; exact semantic fidelity is **not** product-qualified.

### HTML

The library inspector remains available, but HTML is not part of the Zlet Converter v0.1 Markdown conversion route and is outside this product handoff.

## CQS

`zlet-cqs/0.3.0` remains available inside the library for deterministic diagnostic projection and equivalence testing. Handoff v0.1 does **not** authorize CQS as a Zlet Converter acceptance/pass-fail verdict.

Product presentation must keep dimension/coverage evidence and explicit eligibility/status semantics visible if CQS is surfaced later.

## Required product behavior

- run in process; no CLI/subprocess dependency;
- preserve `NOT_APPLICABLE` and `NOT_EVALUATED_*` exactly;
- do not turn quality-evaluation failure into conversion failure;
- expose only the metrics/findings allowed by the qualification profile;
- do not serialize SourceFacts/MarkdownFacts text into ordinary product reports;
- retain exact Quality Core/pipeline identity in the product quality summary;
- treat any Lab ↔ embedded semantic difference as a failed integration gate.

## Regression obligations

The product handoff must retain regression coverage for:

- exact clean control;
- content loss;
- empty output;
- structure loss where qualified;
- order mutation where qualified;
- mixed/insufficient source certainty;
- output-only structural noise (`Alpha` -> `# Alpha` class);
- coverage rounding must never convert real partial evidence to `ELIGIBLE_DOCUMENT_LEVEL`.

## Known limitations

The qualification profile intentionally excludes known interpretation-risk areas rather than calling them correct. The detailed limitations are retained in `qualification.json` and the current metric specification.

Historical Quality Core evidence remains immutable. Any semantic change after this handoff requires a new Quality Core/check/methodology identity and new evidence.
